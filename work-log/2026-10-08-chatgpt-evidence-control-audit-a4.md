- **B19 — Scheduling first/second-step UI.** SS-H07 plus VE-007 00:19–01:04 directly show a calendar first step with month chevrons/date markers/shortcuts and Cancel→Next, then `< PICK DATE` + chosen date summary, `Add Time + ADD` with inline hours/minutes/AM/PM and remove, and Recurring schedule presets/Custom in the second *same-modal* footprint. There is no in-modal custom-calendar first step, Next/Pick Date state transition, or source Add/Remove composition. The persisted date/time and recurrence domain features exist and are validated within their previous scope. **FIX_NOW M5 / SOURCE_STATE_PARITY_OPEN**. - **B20 — Custom nth-weekday month rule is not supported.** VE-009 01:31–01:54 directly shows month selector `Monthly on day 14` vs `Monthly on the 2nd Sunday`, with live summary `every 4 months on the 2nd Sunday`. Original M4 tests validated its documented interval/weekday/date rule subset, not the omitted ordinal capability; do not silently rewrite old PASS as support for B20.
